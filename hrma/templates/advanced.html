Motor Cross-Section

What this shows: 2D cross-sectional view of the hybrid rocket motor geometry

  • Grey: Chamber wall and forward closure (casing)
  • Dark grey: Phenolic liner / insulation
  • Brown: Solid fuel grain
  • Tan/gold: Injector plate (pattern follows selected injector type)
  • Blue-grey: Nozzle wall (throat and expansion)
  • Dashed red: Final port diameter at burnout
  • Cyan: Dimension lines (all measurements in mm)

Performance Analysis Dashboard

What this shows: Comprehensive motor performance metrics

  • Mass Flow Rates: Total, oxidizer, and fuel flow rates (kg/s)
  • Pressure Distribution: Chamber, tank, and injector pressure drop (bar)
  • Regression Rate: Fuel grain burn rate over time (mm/s) with port diameter growth
  • Injector Performance: Exit velocity gauge showing injection effectiveness (m/s)
  • Design validation: All parameters optimized for stable combustion

Thrust and Chamber Pressure vs Time

What this shows: Instantaneous thrust and chamber pressure through the burn, taken from the time-marching solution. In a hybrid the oxidiser flow is constant while the fuel flow follows the regressing port, so the shape of this curve is the O/F shift made visible.

Injector Design Schematic

What this shows: Detailed schematic of the selected injector type (orifice pattern, flow paths and key design dimensions). Hover the elements for parameter explanations.

Altitude Performance

What this shows: How the nozzle performs as ambient pressure drops with altitude

  • Specific Impulse vs Altitude: Isp (s) rises as back pressure falls
  • Thrust vs Altitude: Thrust (N) at the same chamber conditions
  • Thrust Coefficient vs Altitude: Cf, the pure nozzle contribution
  • Atmospheric Pressure vs Altitude: Ambient pressure (bar) used by the model
  • Reading it: The gap between sea-level and vacuum values shows how much the fixed expansion ratio costs off design

Total Impulse vs Altitude

What this shows: Delivered thrust, Isp and impulse efficiency along an altitude sweep

  • Thrust vs Altitude: Thrust (N) at each ambient pressure
  • Specific Impulse vs Altitude: Isp (s) for the same sweep
  • Impulse Efficiency vs Altitude: Percentage of the vacuum impulse actually delivered
  • Applications: Choosing an expansion ratio for the expected flight envelope

Combustion Products Through the Nozzle

What this shows: Mass fraction of the major combustion species at chamber, throat and exit

  • X-axis: Nozzle station (chamber, throat, exit)
  • Y-axis: Mass fraction on a logarithmic scale
  • One line per species: Only species above 0.1 percent are drawn
  • Reading it: Recombination during expansion raises heavy species and lowers radicals such as OH

Combustion Analysis

What this shows: Chemical equilibrium and combustion quality for the selected propellant pair

  • Chemical Equilibrium: Equilibrium species concentrations in the chamber
  • Flame Temperature Profile: Temperature along the chamber axis
  • Combustion Efficiency: Gauge against the 95 percent reference
  • O/F Ratio Optimization: Performance sensitivity around the selected mixture ratio

Operating Point Dashboard

What this shows: The design operating point as gauges, plus the burn-time histories underneath

  • Top two rows: Thrust, chamber pressure, mass flow, temperature, O/F ratio and Isp at the design point
  • Bottom row: Propellant mass, burn rate and port diameter versus time
  • Data source: Time histories come from the transient port-regression solution, not from a placeholder curve
