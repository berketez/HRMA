Map packs (offline detail)

Close-range map detail (roads, buildings, water, place names) for the area around a launch site, from OpenStreetMap; launch site packs also carry a Copernicus Sentinel-2 satellite image (10 m). Nothing is downloaded until you confirm.?A map pack adds OpenStreetMap roads, buildings, water and place names to the globe around one launch site, down to street level (zoom level 15), and works offline once installed. Launch site packs also carry a satellite image under the map lines: a cloud-free mosaic that HRMA made from Copernicus Sentinel-2 scenes, 10 m per pixel, levels 9 to 13 (enlarged above that); places with no clear observation stay empty and show the globe’s own texture. A pack is one file (map data) or two files (map data and satellite image) from HRMA’s own GitHub release; their sizes, source and licences are shown before you confirm. Outside an installed pack’s box, and with no pack installed, the globe looks as before.

Earth rotation is in the flight solution: the flight is solved in a frame turning with the Earth — the Coriolis acceleration from the site latitude acts on the ascent and the descent, and the centrifugal part is inside the local gravity (WGS84). The flight equations use a flat Earth (tangent plane at the pad); the drawn path converts north/east to latitude/longitude with the local radii of curvature, a first-order projection whose error grows with downrange.
