0. Propellant Catalogue

Data source: every catalogue row below states its own reference in the Source column (HRMA propellant catalogue, served by /api/propellants).

How to use: Pick a propellant in the "Propellant (catalogue record)" selector below or click a row here: the choice is the propellant identity sent to the solver and loads that catalogue record into the form. Each row states its own basis in the Basis column (validated or not, and where its c* comes from); not every row is validated.

  • Editable: Every loaded value stays editable - the catalogue only fills the fields.
  • Manual edits are protected: A field you type into is marked as overridden and is never overwritten by a later catalogue pick.
  • Burn rate law: r [m/s] = a x (P [bar])^n - the same convention the solver uses.

Performance Summary

Monte Carlo Analysis (Manufacturing Tolerances)

What it does: Randomly samples the manufacturing tolerances (burn-rate coefficient a, exponent n, density, C*) and derives the statistical distribution of motor performance.

  • Success rate: Percentage of runs that meet the thrust / Isp / peak-pressure acceptance criteria
  • CV%: Coefficient of variation (σ/mean) — a lower value means more consistent manufacturing
  • [p5, p95]: Range that contains 90% of the runs

Parameter Value Unit

Motor Cross-Section

What it shows: 2D axial cross-section of the motor generated from the solver output

  • Chamber: Combustion chamber and grain envelope
  • Throat / Nozzle: Minimum flow area and diverging cone
  • Dimensions: In millimetres (mm)

Performance Analysis Dashboard

What this shows: Propellant mass flow, chamber pressure distribution, the thrust and pressure history, and burn area with Kn over the burn.

Thrust Curve

What this shows: Thrust vs time profile for solid rocket motor burn

  • X-axis: Time (s) from ignition to burnout
  • Y-axis: Thrust (N) - force generated by motor
  • Curve shape: Depends on grain geometry (progressive, regressive, neutral)
  • Peak thrust: Maximum force point (design critical point)
  • Total impulse: Area under curve represents total energy delivery

Chamber Pressure

What this shows: Chamber pressure evolution during motor burn

  • X-axis: Time (s) from ignition to burnout
  • Y-axis: Pressure (bar) inside combustion chamber
  • Pressure profile: Directly related to thrust curve and burn area
  • Design pressure: Maximum operating pressure for safety analysis
  • Pressure ratio: Chamber to ambient pressure ratio
  • Critical points: Ignition transient, steady-state, tail-off regions

3D CAD Design

What this shows: Interactive 3D CAD model of solid rocket motor assembly

  • Gray components: Motor case, nozzle hardware, and structural elements
  • Orange/Red: Solid propellant grain with internal port geometry
  • Blue gradient: Nozzle throat and expansion section
  • Grain geometry: BATES, Star, Wagon Wheel, or End-burner configuration
  • Interactive controls: Rotate (drag), zoom (scroll), pan (shift+drag)
  • Export formats: STEP/STL files for manufacturing and CAD software

Trajectory Analysis
