== Map packs: OpenStreetMap vectors and Copernicus Sentinel-2 imagery (optional download, launch site globe) ==
Data: hrma/static/map/harita_paketleri.json
Pack-Licence: ODbL-1.0
Pack-Licence: Copernicus-Sentinel-Data-Legal-Notice
Licence: Open Data Commons Open Database License 1.0 (ODbL 1.0),
  https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
Attribution: (c) OpenStreetMap contributors. The map shows this attribution on
  screen whenever map pack data is drawn.
Source: Protomaps Basemap planet build 20261115 (version 4.15.2, OpenStreetMap
  replication time 2026-11-15T04:00:00Z), https://build.protomaps.com/; tile
  schema and build tooling by the Protomaps project (github.com/protomaps/basemaps;
  the project states "BSD and ODbL licenses"). Low zoom levels (z0-z5) also carry
  Natural Earth data (public domain).
What ships: the installer contains only the pack list (file names, sizes, sha256).
  The packs themselves are separate files that the user may choose to download
  from the HRMA GitHub release named in that list.
Changes: each pack is a region extract (100 km around a launch site) of the
  source build, z0-z15; the 'landcover' layer (Daylight landcover, derived from
  ESA WorldCover; present only at z0-z7) is removed because it is not
  OpenStreetMap data; all other tiles are byte-identical to the source build.
  The packs are a derivative database and are offered under ODbL 1.0: the pack
  files are published as release assets of the HRMA repository.
Imagery component (launch site packs; listed in the same pack list as a second file
  of the pack, kind "imagery"): a cloud-free true-colour mosaic produced by HRMA
  (tools/harita_goruntu_uret.py) from Copernicus Sentinel-2 Level-2A scenes
  (true-colour image TCI, 10 m, and scene classification SCL, 20 m), found through
  the Element 84 Earth Search STAC catalogue, collection "sentinel-2-l2a", and read
  from the AWS Open Data bucket "sentinel-cogs".
Imagery licence: "Legal notice on the use of Copernicus Sentinel Data and Service
  Information" (European Commission, DG for Internal Market, Industry,
  Entrepreneurship and SMEs; Regulation (EU) No 377/2014 and Commission Delegated
  Regulation (EU) No 1159/2013): free, full and open access; reproduction,
  distribution, communication to the public, adaptation, modification and
  combination with other data and information are permitted. The data is provided
  without any express or implied warranty, including as regards quality and
  suitability for any purpose.
  https://sentinels.copernicus.eu/documents/247904/690755/Sentinel_Data_Legal_Notice
Imagery attribution: "Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data <years>", the
  notice that the legal notice prescribes for adapted or modified data. The years
  and the identifiers of every contributing scene are stored in the imagery file
  (metadata "attribution" and "scenes") and in the pack list; the map shows the
  notice on screen whenever an installed imagery component is in view.
Imagery changes: clouds, cloud shadows and saturated pixels are masked with the
  scene classification; scenes are combined per pixel in a fixed order, colour-
  balanced between acquisition dates on land pixels, resampled to the HRMA globe
  grid (EPSG:4326, 288/2^z degrees per 512 px tile, levels z9-z13) and stored as
  WebP tiles in one SQLite file. Areas without a clear observation are left
  transparent (no synthetic fill).
